Draft plan – __LEAGUE__ – first 6 picks

Pick your slot, then click the player you actually took. Options are ranked by "now" (categories won with the picks so far, of 9); gaps under ~0.1 are ties. If a player is gone, take the next one down.
Boxes = % of the 9 opponents your picks so far beat in each category, comparing your players against each opponent's same number of players. Nothing about later rounds is assumed. “Strong” = 70%+. now = categories won by the picks so far (out of 9). chain = categories won once the best completed chain of picks under that option is added (6 picks vs opponents' 6 picks); hover it to see the chain.
Mark players as Gone as they're taken (type part of a name and press Enter; Shift+Enter = yours) and as Mine when you pick. It re-ranks what's left from your actual roster, so it keeps working if you leave the plan. Ranking = balanced fit on season-total values (games-played aware); only players within the reach window of your next pick are listed.
