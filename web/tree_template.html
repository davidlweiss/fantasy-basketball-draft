Draft plan – first 6 picks

Pick your slot, then click the player you actually took. Children are ranked by expected categories won (of 9); gaps under ~0.1 are ties. If a player is gone, take the next one down.
Boxes = % of the 9 opponents your picks so far beat in each category, comparing your players against each opponent's same number of players. Nothing about later rounds is assumed. “Strong” = 70%+.
The 10 best 6-pick combos for each slot, ranked on the 6 picks alone (vs opponents' first 6 picks), re-scored on fresh simulated drafts. “Emphasize later” = categories where the 6 players sit furthest behind an average team after 6 rounds; the typical fill is guidance only and does not affect the ranking.
