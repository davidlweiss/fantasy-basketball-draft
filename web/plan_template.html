Draft plan – __LEAGUE__

Pick your slot. Options are the players who should still be on the board at your pick, ranked by expected category wins with the roster you actually have (so you can take anyone, not just the top option). Mark players Gone as they go, and Take when you pick. Type part of a name and press Enter to mark it gone (Shift+Enter = yours).
Boxes = chance to beat a typical opponent in each category, comparing your players against what an average team holds after the same number of picks (fixed rosters, projected games played). now = expected category wins (of 9) with your picks plus this player. chain = the same once the best remaining picks through round 6 are added; hover it to see them.
